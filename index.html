HOP is a precision market.

Most prediction markets ask yes or no. HOP asks for a number. Every entry in a pool is scored on how close it lands, how early it was placed and how sure it stayed; your share of the pot is that score times your stake. There are no losers, only distances.

Who we are — on-chain, by the numbers.

We kept getting the direction right and the size wrong, and every market only paid for the direction. So we built the one that pays for the number. HOP runs on Robinhood Chain, keeps the scoring public, and moves USDG with plain transfers you can verify on the explorer.

Our mission: markets priced by precision.

A forecast is a number, not a coin flip. HOP rewards the people who are closest, earliest and most consistent — and shows everyone the leaderboard that proves it.

Built on Robinhood Chain.

Robinhood Chain is an Ethereum L2 built for tokenized stocks: sub-second blocks, fees around a cent and USDG as the settlement dollar. Stakes are plain USDG transfers to the pool treasury, payouts come back the same way, and every one of them is a transaction you can verify.

The stack:

[Robinhood Chain]

The L2 where the pools live. Deposits, payouts and scoring settle here — fast blocks, cent-level gas, real tokenized stocks.

[USDG]

The dollar of every pool. You stake USDG, the pot is USDG, the payout lands as USDG in your wallet.

[Your wallet]

Sign in with the wallet you already own. Your stake sits in the pool treasury only while the pool is open, and the payout comes straight back to that wallet.
